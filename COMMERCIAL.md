# Commercial licensing

scaffold-engine is licensed under the [Business Source License 1.1](./LICENSE). This page explains what that means in practice and how to get a commercial license when you need one.

## What's free

You can use scaffold-engine at no cost, with no license to buy, for:

- **Personal use** — homelab, hobby projects, personal automation.
- **Internal business use** — running it inside your company for your own workflows, on your own hardware.
- **Evaluation** — trying it out to decide whether it fits.
- **Research and education** — academic work, teaching, experiments.

If you're an individual or a team running scaffold-engine for yourselves, you're covered. No strings.

## When you need a commercial license

You need a license from LocketKey LLC if you offer scaffold-engine — or a product or service whose value derives substantially from it — **to third parties for a fee or other commercial advantage**. Concretely:

- Hosting scaffold-engine as a paid (or ad-supported) service for customers.
- Selling a product that embeds or repackages the engine.
- Offering paid consulting deliverables that are substantially a rebranded scaffold-engine deployment.

If you're not sure which side of the line you're on, ask — the answer is free.

## What a commercial license gets you

- The right to offer scaffold-engine commercially, scoped to your use case.
- Priority support directly from the maintainer.
- Input on the roadmap for features your deployment needs.

Terms are scoped per engagement (seat/instance/revenue-based options available) — contact for a quote.

## The Change License

Every version of scaffold-engine automatically converts to the **Apache License 2.0** on its Change Date (four years after release; the current tree converts 2030-08-04). If you can wait, it becomes fully open source on that schedule.

## Support the project without a commercial need

If scaffold-engine saves you time and you just want to support development, GitHub Sponsors works — see the Sponsor button on the repository.
